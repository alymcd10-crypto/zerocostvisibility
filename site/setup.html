Questions

Before you start.

Do I have to post anything?

Very little. We write everything for you. You approve it, then send what we've prepared at the right moments: when a client lists, closes or leaves a review.

Who does the work?

Liz's team builds it. Liz sets the method and reviews your plan before we hand it over.

Does it work outside Illinois?

Yes. It's built around your towns, your groups and your clients, wherever you sell.

What about my brokerage's marketing rules?

You approve every template before it goes out, so you can check each one against your brokerage's rules first.

What happens after 30 days?

You run it. Or ask us about Visibility Management, where we keep it running for you each month.

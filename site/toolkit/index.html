Step 1 of 3

Give clients the post

Don't ask sellers to share the listing. Give them the words. On listing day, text your seller all five options and tell them which one sounds most like them. Buyers can post three times: on keys day, once they've settled in, and after their first big project. Swap in your name and number before you send.

School and neighborhood

Seller
Our beautiful home is on the market. [Neighborhood and school highlight, e.g. Oak Grove schools and an amazing neighborhood]. To set up a private showing, call [Your name] (our realtor) at [your phone].

Best for family communities and school-district markets.

Top local expert

Seller
Now available in the heart of [town or neighborhood]. For a private showing, contact top local expert [Your name] at [your phone].

Best for walkable downtowns and lifestyle listings.

Shortest

Seller
Please call my agent, [Your name], for a private showing at [your phone].

Best for clients who don't want to write much.

The personal story

Seller
We bought this home seven years ago and it has been everything we hoped for. Time for the next chapter. If you or someone you love is looking in [town], message me, or call our realtor [Your name] at [your phone].

Best for longtime owners. Highest engagement of the five.

Before it goes live

Seller
Coming to the market in [town] next week: [X bed, Y bath, key feature]. If you know anyone looking, DM me or reach out directly to [Your name] at [your phone]. Happy to do a private walkthrough first.

Best for creating scarcity. Brings DMs before it hits the MLS.

Keys day

Buyer
We did it. After [length of search] we finally found our home in [town]. So grateful to [Your name] with [your team and brokerage] for finding it and getting our offer accepted in a multiple-offer situation. If you're looking in the area, [Your name] is the one to call: [your phone].

Best for buyers who beat out multiple offers.

New to town

Buyer
Officially residents of [town]. We're so excited to be here. Huge thank-you to our realtor [Your name] for making the move feel manageable from across the country. If anyone has restaurant, preschool or contractor recommendations, send them our way. And if you're house-hunting here, our realtor is the one: [your phone].

Best for relocation buyers. Doubles as a we-belong-here post.

The renovation reveal

Buyer
Six months in our [town] home and the [kitchen / yard / basement] is finally done. Sharing because so many of you helped, including [Your name], who connected us with [contractor] the week we moved in. Still recommending [Your name] to anyone who'll listen: [your phone].

Best 6 to 12 months after closing. A second wave from the same client.

Step 2 of 3

The 90-second ask

When someone posts "anyone know a realtor?" in a local Facebook group, text three to five past clients who live there. Turn on post alerts for 8 to 10 community groups and keep your past clients sorted by neighborhood, so you can send the ask within 90 seconds.

Your closest clients

Text
(Send a screenshot of the post) Please chime in if you can.

For your top 10 to 15 raving fans. They don't need context.

Recent clients

Text
(Send a screenshot of the post) Would you mind chiming in on this group if you can?

For clients you closed with in the last 12 months.

Long-ago clients

Text
Hi [name], saw this post in [group] and thought of you. Would you mind dropping a quick comment if you're up for it? Totally no pressure either way.

For clients from 2+ years ago. Warmth plus a reminder of who you are.

When they ask how to help

In person
Honestly? The most valuable thing you could do is post about our experience on your Facebook page. Tag me if you're comfortable, or just mention me by name. Those community posts are how people find me.

For when the client offers first. Always say: make the post.

Step 3 of 3

Ask for the review, then reuse it

Send it the week of closing, while they're still excited about the keys. If you haven't heard back, follow up on days 5, 10 and 14.

Then use every review everywhere

  1. Screenshot the review, cropped to the stars and first paragraph.
  2. Add your branding and the date within 48 hours.
  3. Post it on at least six channels over 30 days: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, your email signature, your Google profile and your newsletter.

Review request by text or email

❤️ If you were happy with our help with your real estate experience, would you mind leaving us a review on both Google and Zillow? Reviews truly help my business and help future buyers and sellers find us! If it helps when writing your review, I’d love if you mentioned the town you bought or sold in, whether I helped you buy or sell, and anything you appreciated about the experience—such as my negotiation, pricing strategy, marketing, communication, responsiveness, local market knowledge, or help navigating the process. If phrases like “best Realtor” or “local real estate expert” genuinely describe your experience, those are wonderful to include too! Please use your own words—personal details about your experience are always the most meaningful. 😊 ⭐ Zillow: [Your Zillow review link] ⭐ Google: [Your Google review link] Thank you so much for taking the time to do this. We truly appreciate your business, your trust, and your support! ❤️

Swap in your own Zillow and Google review links before you send.

Bonus

Showing up in AI search

More buyers now ask ChatGPT who the best realtor in their town is. What it says depends on what it can find about you online, so make that easy to find.

  1. Use the same bio everywhere

    Put one sentence on Zillow, Google, your brokerage page and LinkedIn: [Name] is a [role] serving [specific towns, not "Chicagoland"], specializing in [property type]. Closed [$X] in [year].

  2. Get reviews that name places

    Ask clients to mention the town, the neighborhood, the type of home and the problem you solved. The review request above already asks for this.

  3. Tidy up your website

    Add LocalBusiness schema, list each service area in its own sentence, add an FAQ with questions buyers really ask, and embed your Google reviews.

  4. Check every quarter

    Ask ChatGPT and Claude "best realtor in [your town]" and "[your name] realtor reviews." If you're not in the answer, start with whatever's missing.

For every past client

The first year after closing

Six touches, timed from each client's closing date. None of them ask for anything.

  1. Week of closing

    A closing gift

    Hand-delivered, not mailed. Pick something they'll want to photograph and post.

  2. Day 30

    A check-in text

    "One month in. How's the house?" Listen, solve one thing, and connect them with a contractor if they need one.

  3. Month 3

    Something useful

    "Just heard about [tax appeal deadline / school open house / new restaurant] and thought of you."

  4. Month 6

    A coffee invitation

    "It's been six months. Free for coffee this week?" Most people say no, and that's fine. The invitation is the point.

  5. Month 9

    A seasonal home tip

    "Reminder to drain your outdoor spigots this week," or the name of the gutter person you use.

  6. Month 12

    An anniversary card

    Handwritten and mailed. "A year ago today you got the keys. I'm here whenever you need me."

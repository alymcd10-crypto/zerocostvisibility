Privacy

Last updated November 2026

This site is run by Liz Turnbaugh Federman, a broker with The Leslie McDonnell Team at RE/MAX Suburban in Libertyville, Illinois. Questions about this page go to liz@listingsbyleslie.com.

What I collect

  • Contact and booking details you type into a form: your name, email, phone if you give it, brokerage and town, and your message.
  • Your email and where you heard about the toolkit, when you unlock the attendee toolkit.
  • Usage data from Google Analytics, such as the pages you visit and the link or QR code that brought you here.

Cal.com handles session scheduling and Stripe handles payment. They collect what they need to do that, under their own privacy policies. I don't see or store your card number.

Why I collect it

To reply to you, run your session, send you the toolkit and a few follow-up emails, and see which pages are useful so I can improve the site.

How long I keep it

Until you ask me to delete it.

Your choices

You can opt out of emails at any time by replying and asking me to stop. You can ask me to delete your information by emailing liz@listingsbyleslie.com.

What I don't do

I don't sell your data.

## 8. Things to keep in mind

- **Google review rules.** The review request opens with "If you were happy…" and suggests phrases such as "best Realtor". Google's policy forbids asking only happy clients and suggesting wording. The v7 notes soften this ("I give them ideas, never a script"), but the message itself has not changed.
- **"Booked Solid"** is the title of a well-known business book and may be a registered trademark. Worth a check before the title is printed on anything.
- **The 2026 total.** An earlier verified figure was $38.61M. The deck uses $40.1M. Liz should confirm which is right.
- **"Since 1984".** A directory listing gives that founding year for RD Training Systems. It was not confirmed from their own site, so the site does not say it.
- **AI-made images.** The stage photo is not from a real event. It should not be captioned as one.
- **Starpower.** Liz attends it; she does not speak at it. The word survives only as the attendee code and the `/starpower` short link, because both are already printed in the deck.
